* `RCCL_SDMA_LEND_ENABLE` (default `0`): opt in to the SDMA mesh-lending AllReduce. On a single-node two-rank communicator on gfx942 or gfx950 whose GPUs share a direct xGMI link, a sum of `float32`, `float16` or `bfloat16` moves through SDMA engines of the two ranks and of the GPUs outside the communicator (the lenders). The call synchronizes the caller's stream and is not used under graph capture.
* `RCCL_SDMA_LEND_MIN_BYTES` (default 4 MiB) and `RCCL_SDMA_LEND_MAX_BYTES` (default 256 MiB): message sizes that take the SDMA mesh-lending AllReduce. Each rank allocates two inboxes of `RCCL_SDMA_LEND_MAX_BYTES`.
* `RCCL_SDMA_LEND_MAX_LENDERS` (default `7`): most GPUs a two-rank communicator borrows SDMA engines from. Without a lender the path is off; `0` keeps it on over the direct link alone, for measurements.
