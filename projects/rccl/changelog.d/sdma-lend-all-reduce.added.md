* `RCCL_SDMA_LEND_ENABLE` (default `0`): opt in to the SDMA mesh-lending AllReduce. On a single-node two-rank communicator on gfx942 or gfx950 whose GPUs share a direct xGMI link, a sum of `float32`, `float16` or `bfloat16` moves each rank's input to the peer through SDMA engines of the rank's own GPU and of the GPUs outside the communicator (the lenders), then one kernel per rank adds. Plain device buffers, no window registration. The call synchronizes the caller's stream before it posts the copies.
* `RCCL_SDMA_LEND_MIN_BYTES` (default 4 MiB) and `RCCL_SDMA_LEND_MAX_BYTES` (default 256 MiB): message sizes that take the SDMA mesh-lending AllReduce. Each rank allocates two inboxes of `RCCL_SDMA_LEND_MAX_BYTES`.
* `RCCL_SDMA_LEND_LENDERS` (default `auto`): GPUs whose SDMA engines a two-rank communicator may borrow. `auto` takes every GPU that ROCr shows the process and that has an xGMI link to both ranks, and a comma list of PCI addresses (`0000:05:00.0,...`) names them. Without a lender the path is off; `none` on both ranks keeps it on over the direct link alone, for measurements.
